# Ceylon Roots Cyprus — sample shop

Open `dist/index.html` in a browser to view the sample store. The photos and all scripts are included, so it also works offline.

Includes responsive product catalogue, category/search/price filters, sorting, product details, shopping bag and sample checkout. No orders are submitted and no payments are collected. The bag resets on reload. Company details, product specifications, prices and delivery terms are illustrative and should be replaced before launch.

Product image sources, creators and licenses are in `dist/assets/credits.json`, and accessible from the Photo credits link in the website footer. Photographs have been resized for web display and are cropped by the layout. Adapted photographs retain their stated source license; the photograph licenses do not apply to the entire website source.
